# DineHop product facts

This page is the factual public reference for DineHop Restaurant Finder. It was last reviewed on 16 August 2026.

## Identity

- **Product name:** DineHop
- **Full product name:** DineHop Restaurant Finder
- **Category:** Restaurant discovery and night-out planning
- **Official website:** [dinehop.app](https://dinehop.app)
- **Google Play:** [restaurant.app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=restaurant.app)
- **Contact:** [contact@dinehop.app](mailto:contact@dinehop.app)

## Availability

- **Android:** Available on Google Play.
- **iOS:** Under development. No public release date has been announced.
- **Geographic coverage:** Restaurant discovery works worldwide.
- **Price:** Free, supported by advertising. There is no subscription.

## Languages

DineHop supports ten interface languages:

- English
- German
- Spanish
- French
- Italian
- Portuguese
- Japanese
- Korean
- Indonesian
- Arabic, including right-to-left layout

## Core capabilities

- Search by dish, cuisine, venue name, neighbourhood, postcode or area.
- Browse nearby restaurants and food categories.
- Compare menus, prices, opening hours, photos, ratings and reviews.
- Filter and sort restaurant results.
- View official food-hygiene information where a supported public scheme provides it.
- Save places with private notes.
- Search, filter and sort saved places.
- Create named multi-stop outing plans called Hops.
- Read community reviews and publish a review with an account.
- Open directions, phone numbers, websites and menu links.

## Food-hygiene and inspection coverage

DineHop uses official government data in eight supported countries. Coverage is country-wide in the United Kingdom, France, Singapore, Finland, Denmark and Norway. Coverage in the United States and Canada is currently limited to the cities listed below.

| Country | Current geographic coverage | Public authority or scheme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | Country-wide where published records are available | Food Standards Agency FHRS and Scottish FHIS records |
| France | Country-wide | Alim'confiance |
| Singapore | Country-wide | Singapore Food Agency grades |
| Finland | Country-wide | Oiva |
| Denmark | Country-wide | Danish Smiley scheme |
| Norway | Country-wide | Smilefjes |
| United States | New York City, Chicago and Austin only | NYC restaurant grades, Chicago Food Inspections and Austin Public Health scores |
| Canada | Toronto only | DineSafe |

Coverage is shown only when DineHop can match a venue to a supported official record. A missing rating does not mean that a venue has failed an inspection.

## Accounts and personal content

An account is not required for basic restaurant search and browsing. An account is required for features that save or publish personal content, including synced saved places, private notes and community reviews.

Users can manage or delete their account from the app. Instructions are also available on the [account deletion page](https://dinehop.app/delete-account).

## Data sources

Restaurant details, maps, photos and some reviews are supplied by third parties, including Google Maps. Food-hygiene information comes from the relevant official authority. Community reviews are submitted by DineHop users.

Source attribution is displayed in the product where required. Third-party information can change and may not always be complete or current.

## What DineHop does not currently provide

- Table reservations
- Food ordering or delivery
- An available iOS application
- A public application programming interface
- Public access to the application source code
