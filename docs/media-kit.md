# DineHop media kit

This page provides approved factual descriptions and references for journalists, reviewers, directory maintainers and other people writing about DineHop.

## Official descriptions

### Short

DineHop is an Android restaurant discovery and night-out planning app for searching, comparing and organising places, with official food-hygiene ratings where available.

### Medium

DineHop helps people find restaurants by dish, cuisine or area, compare menus, prices, opening hours, photos and reviews, and check official food-hygiene information where supported. Places can be saved with private notes or grouped into a named multi-stop plan called a Hop. DineHop is available on Android, with iOS under development.

### Full

DineHop is a restaurant discovery and night-out planning app. It searches for places by dish, cuisine, venue name, neighbourhood, postcode or area, then brings together practical information such as menus, prices, opening hours, photos, ratings and reviews. In supported jurisdictions, DineHop also shows the venue's rating from the relevant official food-hygiene or inspection scheme. Users can save places with private notes, search their saved collection and group several venues into a named multi-stop outing plan called a Hop. DineHop is available on Android through Google Play. An iOS version is under development, with no announced release date.

## Accurate positioning

DineHop is a restaurant discovery and planning product. It does not currently provide table reservations or food delivery. Do not describe the iOS application as available until DineHop announces its release.

For detailed coverage and data-source information, use the [product facts](product-facts.md).
