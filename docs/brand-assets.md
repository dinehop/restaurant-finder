# DineHop brand and media assets

The files in this repository are provided so that people can identify DineHop accurately in reviews, articles, directories and technical references.

## Available files

### Brand

- `assets/brand/dinehop-logo.svg`: scalable DineHop logo.
- `assets/brand/dinehop-icon.png`: 512 by 512 pixel app icon.
- `assets/brand/social-preview.jpg`: landscape product image for links and articles.

### Promotional product screens

- `assets/screenshots/home.webp`: restaurant search and categories.
- `assets/screenshots/search-results.webp`: restaurant results and comparison details.
- `assets/screenshots/map.webp`: map-based discovery.
- `assets/screenshots/filters.webp`: cuisine and dietary filters.
- `assets/screenshots/place-details.webp`: restaurant details.
- `assets/screenshots/hygiene-rating.webp`: official hygiene information on a venue.

### Raw product screens

The [raw screenshot collection](../assets/screenshots/raw/) contains direct 1080 by 2340 pixel Android captures without device frames, headlines or decorative backgrounds. It includes Home, Search, Results, Place details, Reviews, Saved places and Settings screens in English.

## Permitted editorial use

The logo, icon and screenshots may be reproduced without charge when the purpose is to identify, review, discuss or link to DineHop, subject to the repository's [licence terms](../LICENSE.md).

Please:

- Use the name `DineHop` with the same capitalisation.
- Keep the logo's proportions and colours unchanged.
- Use the supplied files rather than redrawing the mark.
- Preserve visible Google Maps and other third-party attribution in screenshots.
- Make clear that screenshots show the DineHop product interface.
- Link to [dinehop.app](https://dinehop.app) where a source link is appropriate.

Please do not:

- Imply endorsement, sponsorship or partnership without written permission.
- Use the DineHop name or mark as another product's identity.
- Place the logo inside another logo or alter its colours and geometry.
- Crop attribution or source information out of a screenshot.
- Use the assets on merchandise or in paid advertising without written permission.

Resizing and minor cropping are acceptable when they do not distort the asset, remove attribution or change its meaning.

## Third-party content

Some screenshots include maps, venue details, photos, ratings or reviews supplied by third parties. The permission in this repository applies to DineHop's own presentation and branding only. It does not grant separate rights to third-party content.
